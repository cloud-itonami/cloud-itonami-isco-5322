# Business Model: Independent Home-Based Care Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-5322`
- ISCO-08: `5322`
- Occupation: Home-based Personal Care Workers
- Social impact: aging-in-place, disability-support, caregiver-relief

## Customer

- elderly and disabled clients and families
- home-care agencies
- insurers

## Offer

- consented intake and care scheduling
- scheduled home visits
- daily-living assist documentation
- caregiver relief scheduling
- outcome reporting

## Revenue

- per-visit fee
- monthly care-plan fee
- insurance/benefit billing support fee

## Trust Controls

- no lifting/transfer assist without governor gate
- urgent-risk escalation cannot be suppressed
- client data disclosure requires consent and purpose
